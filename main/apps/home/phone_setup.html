Color pocket cardsUp to 6

Save up to 6 cards or QR codes. On the home screen press the up key to open, up/down keys to page, and OK to return. A 3:4 ratio is recommended; keep white margins around QR codes.

In the file picker, long-press an image to enter multi-select, then tick the other images and confirm. JPEG, PNG or WebP images are accepted.

Use the photo library instead

If the photo library only allows single selection, use the multi-select entry above; you can save images to the phone's "Files" first. Open the setup page by typing http://192.168.4.1 in the system browser's address bar to avoid restrictions of some built-in browsers.

Each selected image is scaled to 240 × 320 and compressed; up to 24 KB each, 144 KB for all 6. Replace, remove and reorder are saved when you finish setup.
